### Queues has a property of FIFO, first in first out. Meaning that the first data enters the structure should be the first that leaves as they lasted the longer. Enqueue means to get in, and dequeue means to get out of the line. We can impelment this in a simple way by declaring a data struct with a list and a integar tracker of current size, however the list itself needs a predeclared number as its maximum size, this means that the queues itself cannot grow with the data amount. We also need to track the which is the current last data to be push out next in the queue for the FIFO feature.
